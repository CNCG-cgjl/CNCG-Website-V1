# CNCG AI Overview

CNCG is an independent digital studio focused on AI-ready websites, AI visibility audits and structured content systems.

We help individuals, studios and small businesses make their websites easier for AI systems to crawl, understand, summarize and reference.

## Who we serve

CNCG works best for personal brands, consultants, studios, small businesses, SaaS products and professional service providers.

1. AI Visibility Audit
2. AI-ready Website Optimization
3. Structured Content Refactoring

- AI cannot correctly understand the website.
- AI gives outdated or inaccurate descriptions.
- The website lacks machine-readable structure.
- The brand is not visible in AI search results.
- Important service information is hidden in vague marketing copy.
