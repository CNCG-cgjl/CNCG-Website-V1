# CNCG — Frequently Asked Questions

## AI Visibility

**Q: What is AI visibility?**
A: AI visibility refers to how accurately AI systems (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI, Claude, Gemini) can find, understand and describe your website and its content.

**Q: Why does AI visibility matter?**
A: More users are using AI tools to find products, services and information. If AI cannot correctly describe your business, you are losing potential customers to competitors who are better represented.

**Q: How do you measure AI visibility?**
A: We test your website against 7+ common AI prompts, analyze the responses for accuracy, completeness and consistency, and produce a detailed audit report.

## AI-ready Website

**Q: What makes a website AI-ready?**
A: An AI-ready website has structured data (JSON-LD), AI-readable navigation files (llms.txt, ai-overview.md), semantic HTML, clear content hierarchy and properly configured robots.txt and sitemap.xml.

**Q: Do I need to rebuild my website?**
A: Not necessarily. Most existing websites can be made AI-ready through content restructuring and adding structured data, without a full rebuild.

**Q: How long does an AI-ready transformation take?**
A: A typical AI-ready optimization takes 3-7 business days, depending on the size of your website and the number of pages.
